Rwanda Addressing System
This is a testing platform for the National addressing System for Rwanda it consists in defining the bounding box based on Rwanda centroid point and a 4x4 recursive squares and the principles and logic behind.  
The proposed Rwanda National Addressing System introduces a structured, standardized, and geospatially enabled approach to identifying and locating every addressable object within the country

The proposed addressing framework is guided by the following principles:
National Coverage: The system shall provide complete and consistent coverage of Rwanda's territory, including urban, peri-urban, rural, and future development areas.
 Uniqueness: Every addressable object shall receive a unique identifier that remains stable over time.
 Scalability: The framework shall accommodate population growth, urban expansion, new developments, and future technological requirements.
 Interoperability: The system shall integrate seamlessly with Geographic Information Systems (GIS), navigation platforms, government databases, emergency services, logistics systems, and digital government platforms.
Hybrid Human-Machine Design: A pure grid-based system offers technical efficiency, while human-readable addresses support operational usability. Therefore, the proposed framework adopts a hybrid approach where the geospatial grid serves as the machine layer and human-readable addresses form the user layer.
The addressing framework uses geographic coordinates as its primary spatial reference mechanism. The National Geospatial Grid Framework, the foundation of the system is a hierarchical geospatial grid. The country is divided into progressively smaller spatial units represented by unique alphanumeric identifiers.
The grid approach provides: Consistent national coverage, Uniform address generation, High spatial precision, Efficient machine processing, Independence from street naming availability
The proposed grid framework is based on: Rwanda Bounding Box but modified to be presented as exact square at its center the Rwanda centroid point (29.880, -1.944). The Reference Systems: ITRF 2005, WGS84 (EPSG:4326).
