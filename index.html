kigali.RAI.Grid-based Geocode

Click into the grid, one fold at a time.

The 4×4 RAIRWanda Adress Identifier grid is drawn right on the map. Click a cell and the map zooms in to fill it with the same grid again — Eight folds narrow any point down to an 8-character code. Or type a code into the panel to watch it unfold in reverse.
